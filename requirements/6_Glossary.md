# Glossary

Terms as used in the kata brief. Protocols, edge design, and AI ops words belong in ADRs or [3_NFRs.md](3_NFRs.md), not here.

| Term | Definition |
|:--|:--|
| Von Digitalis Estates | Large, sprawling estate the 72nd Countess wants to monetise. |
| 72nd Countess Von Digitalis | Owner; inherited after a “bizarre gardening accident” (204th in line). |
| Historic rides | 40 eighteenth-century amusement rides; recently passed inspection after asbestos, glass, and gnomes were removed. |
| Exotic and poisonous animal collection | Previously private; opening to the public; 200+ aquatic and land animals across 55 displays and enclosures. |
| Display / enclosure | The 55 animal spaces named in the brief. |
| Jumping pirana / piranha | Colony that needs population checks. The brief spells *pirana*; usual English is *piranha*. |

| Carnivorous plant collection | What the family may have to sell if visitation does not reach at least 15,000/day within three years. |
| Garden gnomes | Previous family business; “unfortunately highly explosive”; no longer viable. |
| Patchy Wi-Fi | Estate connectivity as stated in the brief. |
| MQTT-capable hardware | The brief assumes budget to install such devices in the park. Where they sit is an ADR. |
