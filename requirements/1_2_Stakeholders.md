# Stakeholders

Roles implied by the kata brief. The brief names the Countess; other titles are inferred from “invest & deploy staff” and animal care.

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|:--|:--|
| Countess (estate owner) | Profit; grow from ~5,000 to ≥15,000 visitors/day in three years or risk selling the carnivorous plants; know where to invest; healthy animals; more returning visitors. |
| Guests | Buy tickets, including family passes; visit the 40 rides and the newly public animal collection. |
| Staff who deploy people / invest | Need a popularity signal so placement and spend are not guesswork. |
| Staff who look after animals | Health, feeding (how much / how well), jumping-piranha population. |
| Staff who run the 40 rides | Inferred from historic rides that passed inspection. |
| Kata judges | Repo they can navigate; AI on the three challenges; constraints honoured. See [7_Kata expectations.md](7_Kata%20expectations.md). |
