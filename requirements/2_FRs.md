# Functional Requirements

> Note:   
> Core guest, operations, and commercial capabilities are specified in [Appendix A](Appendix%20A_%20Core%20functionality.md). AI scenarios are expanded in [Appendix B](Appendix%20B_%20AI%20scenarios%20explained.md).

1. FR#1. Provide the core estate platform (ticketing & access, ops intranet, ride/asset maintenance, animal care, popularity instrumentation). See [Appendix A](Appendix%20A_%20Core%20functionality.md).

2. Enable the below scenarios of automating data processing and data exchange (AI-based):

| Area of application | FR# | Functional requirements | Data input | Data output | Tech Choice | Comments |
|:--|:--:|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Yield & experiments | 2A | Async dynamic pricing within human-approved floors/ceilings. Prices are not computed in the checkout hot path. | SKU, channel, occupancy, weather, daypart, experiment bucket, historical conversion/yield | Published price list / variant for POS, web, kiosk | Hybrid (ML, Code) | Failed cloud = last-known-good prices at the edge. Never A/B safety-critical access prices to zero or above a hard ceiling. |
| Yield & experiments | 2B | A/B (and multivariate) experiments on family-pass composition, bundles, daypart surcharges, return vouchers vs membership. | Party id / membership / device, assignment snapshot, offer shown, purchase, redemption | Experiment id, variant, assignment, conversion and yield metrics | Code + stats | Assignment must be sticky for the visit and available **offline** (ticket payload or local flag snapshot). |
| Yield & experiments | 2C | AI cohort / demographic analysis of experiment and visit outcomes - who responded, where they went, what to do next. | Ticket type, declared party structure, opted-in membership attributes, zone/ride events, spend, variant | Cohort insights, segment lift, recommended next experiment or offer | ML, GenAI | Declared attributes are CRM truth. Inferred demographics never silently become identity records. Privacy and eval required. |
| Popularity & flow | 2D | Measure popularity of rides, zones, and enclosures (entries, throughput, dwell) so investment and staffing are evidence-based. | MQTT occupancy / gate counts, ride cycle counts, ticket scans, dwell at exhibits | Heat map, popularity rank, dwell, throughput vs capacity | Code, time-series | Primary answer to “we have no idea what is popular”. Degrade gracefully when MQTT is gapped. |
| Popularity & flow | 2E | Predict congestion / flow 30–90 minutes ahead. | Live occupancy, ride status, weather, event calendar, ticket mix (e.g. family-pass share) | Predicted queue/occupancy by zone with confidence | ML | Compare predicted vs actual counts continuously. Hold out days for verification. |
| Popularity & flow | 2F | Recommend staff deployment and (offline) investment priorities from popularity + predicted load + downtime cost. | 2D/2E outputs, roster, ride criticality, yield-at-risk | Staffing suggestions; “where to invest” ranked list with evidence | Hybrid (ML, Code) | Advisory. Duty manager accepts/rejects. Combine downtime of a popular ride with lost tickets/hour. |
| Guest experience | 2G | Personalized / A/B walking itineraries and next-best-experience for guests (especially returning visitors). | Opt-in location or checkpoint scans, wait times, ticket entitlements, past visits, experiment variant | Suggested route / “start here”, skip-the-queue hints | Hybrid (ML, GenAI, Code) | No unsafe shortcuts through animal areas. Guest thumbs + offline metrics. Works as static “start here” cards when connectivity is poor. |
| Guest experience | 2H | Return-visit / win-back offers (next-best-visit) after the guest leaves. | Visit history, experiment outcomes, membership opt-in, cohort from 2C | Offer, channel, send-time, cap | ML, Code | Frequency caps and opt-out. This is the loyalty lever; it is not spam. |
| Animal care | 2I | Detect health and feeding anomalies (how much / how well animals eat) across 55 displays. | Keeper observations, feed amounts/refusals, environment sensors (temp, water quality), optional cameras | Alert with confidence, evidence, suggested action | ML, optional Vision | High recall first. Keeper accept/reject is the training signal. Never autonomous welfare decisions. |
| Animal care | 2J | Estimate jumping-piranha population (colony-level). | Feeder events, camera/sonar if installed, keeper census | Population estimate ± interval, trend, breeding/loss flags | Vision / ML | Periodic human census is ground truth. Kata-specific; must have an eval harness. |
| Ride maintenance | 2K | Predictive maintenance and inspect-before-peak alerts for historic rides and estate assets. | MQTT heartbeats (vibration, motor, gates, e-stop), cycle counts, inspection due, work-order history | Anomaly flag, recommended inspect-by time, work order draft | ML, Code | False-positive rate vs missed failure is the eval pair. Popular-ride downtime should surface yield-at-risk. |
| Ops intranet | 2L | Copilot for keepers and duty managers: grounded answers from SOPs, keeper notes, and live state. | Staff question, estate docs, live alerts | Answer with citations; no invented vet doses or safety overrides | GenAI (RAG) + Code | Grounded retrieval only. Citations required. Fail closed to “ask the vet / duty manager”. |

3. FR#3. Automate associated ML/AI functionality operations and data management: model versioning, golden-case evals, drift/accuracy monitoring, shadow mode before autonomy, human-in-the-loop routing, and vendor-abstraction so a model or provider change does not rewrite the estate platform.

> Note: More detailed explanation is available in [Appendix B: AI scenarios explained](Appendix%20B_%20AI%20scenarios%20explained.md).
>
> AI is **advisory and experimental**. Core ticketing, entitlements, and gate access stay deterministic. Safety closures, animal-welfare thresholds, and evacuation messages are never A/B tested or model-overridden.
